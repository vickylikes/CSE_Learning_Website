Ee Website ni Computer Science (CSE) ni College ki leda Computer Centre ki vellakunda, own ga nerchukovalani anukune vaallakosam build chesam. Alage memu nerchukune process lo maaku experience vastundi kada ani kuda chesam. Indhulo Menu lo starting nunchi memu emem chadivamo, ela nerchukunnamo, maaku vachina mistakes enti anevi anni post chestham. Kabatti idhi oka Guide laga untundi. Website lo content antha English lone untundi. English sarigga raakapothe konchem kastam avutundi. Pedda paragraphs untayi, chala content untundi, fast ga chadivi important points ni burra lo pettukovali, opika undali. Lecture videos lo English fast ga matladithe Playback Speed ni 0.75x cheskuni vinali. Ilaanti chala vishayalu untayi. Kabatti maa Website tho CSE ni sonthanga nerchukovalante munduga konni basic vishayalu telisi undali.

Munduga koncham high-level English teliste better, especially lecture videos kosam. Spoken English classes ki velladam kanna Terminator, Mission Impossible laanti English movies ni Telugu dubbed version tho paatu English subtitles petkoni choodadam, alage Cengage Mathematics laanti English books chadavadam useful ga untayi. Rendavadi Computer Basics teliyali, ante files mariyu folders ni handle cheyyadam, Google lo browsing cheyyadam laantivi ravali. Moodavadi koncham programming language gurinchi idea undali. Best ga C tho start cheyyadam. "LET US C" ane oka book undi. Adhi konchem outdated ayina ippatiki useful ga untundi. Daani Yashwanth Kanetkar gaaru rasaru. Memu aa book ni complete ga chadavaledu, just 5 leda 6 chapters varaku chadivam. Ee Introduction lo memu peddaga emi rayaledu; main content antha "Introduction_English" lo untundi.
